01

Executive summary

The Address Investments runs its sales recruiting on Leila AI — a candidate sourcing and scoring system built by Karim AlAkkad, HRIS Lead, on his own initiative. It works. It has processed 5,140 candidates, it encodes six months of ratified hiring criteria, and it was the basis of a CEO and Managing Director briefing.

It also runs on one laptop, keeps candidate personal data in Google Sheets and on a third-party host, acquires much of its pipeline through browser automation against platforms that forbid it, and records a hiring stage for 15 of its 5,140 candidates. None of those four things survives contact with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law, which becomes enforceable in October 2026.

This document specifies the platform that replaces it: the same criteria, the same tiers, the same rulings — moved onto owned infrastructure, with a real database, enforced pipeline stages, consent as a precondition for contact, and a complete audit trail behind every verdict. Sourcing shifts from scraping to inbound: job posts on LinkedIn, TikTok and Facebook carry a link to our own application page, where a candidate uploads a CV and we extract and prefill it.

The organising principle

Deterministic rules decide. Language models order and explain. A candidate's tier is always traceable to a rule and a number — never to a model output — because that is what a rejected applicant is entitled to ask for, and what the regulator is entitled to see.

Two findings outrank the stage gap. This is not a defect in the code, and Leila's own documentation says so. A stage is a spreadsheet cell nobody is compelled to fill. The platform makes a stage transition the only way to move a candidate, which converts the funnel from a manual tally into a query.

2.3 What must be preserved

The scoring logic is the most valuable asset in the handover, because it encodes rulings that cost real money to learn:

  • The Shorouk bug. A substring test matched "uk" inside "Shorouk" and hard-disqualified every El Shorouk candidate at score 0 for weeks. It had been recorded as a missing allow-list entry — the wrong cause. Fixed 3 Aug 2026 by anchoring to word boundaries.
  • The 4 August re-scale. Correcting a 115-point scale to a true 100 moved 1,518 candidates between tiers and cut P1 from 775 to 331. Priority counts before and after that date are not comparable.
  • Seniority is not management. A Senior Property Consultant is a target; a Senior Sales Manager is disqualified. Only actual management scope disqualifies.
  • Team Leaders and Supervisors are routed, not rejected — out of entry-level scoring and into the tenured track.
  • Own staff are never sourced. Matched on current employer and current title only — a past stint makes someone a rehire candidate, which is a different case. 38 rows excluded; 6 had been P1.

Every one of these must survive migration byte-identically, and be provable.

06

Solution principles

6.1 The evaluation stack

Leila's scorer is entirely deterministic — there is no model anywhere in it. Language models are additive here, and strictly subordinate:

Layer 0

Hard gates — deterministic

Outside Greater Cairo, age outside band, managerial title, current employee, eleven or more years. Rule expressions over structured fields. No model call. Failure ends the evaluation and costs nothing.

Layer 1

Component score — deterministic

Location 30, sales fit 25, entry level 20, education 15, contact 10; bonuses applied on top and capped at 100. This layer alone assigns the tier. Fully reproducible, fully auditable.

Layer 2

Soft criteria — model-scored

Only what rules cannot read: CV narrative and summary. One isolated call per criterion returning a score and a verbatim evidence span. Orders candidates within a tier and can raise a review flag. It can never cross a tier boundary or reverse a gate.

Layer 3

Similarity — tiebreaker

Vector retrieval for shortlist ordering only. Never affects a verdict.

6.2 Standing rules become schema

Three rules Leila carries as written policy are implemented as structure, so compliance stops depending on anyone remembering:

Rule todayImplemented as
Never invent candidate data; blank beats guessedEvery candidate field stores its own value, source, verified-at and verified-by. Unverified is the default and is visible. A guessed value has nowhere to live
Unverified candidates are never contacted or shortlistedVerification state is a precondition on outreach and shortlist queries, enforced in code
No assumptions — surface ambiguity, never resolve it quietlyA first-class borderline verdict and a review queue. Unattended runs record the ambiguity and safely do nothing

6.3 Processing flow

source record → immutable raw capture (hash-keyed, original bytes retained) → normalise → identity resolution / de-duplication → extract (text layer | OCR) → sanitise → structured profile → profile summary → embed → vector index → Layer 0 gates ──[fail]──→ evaluation (FAIL) → review queue → end → Layer 1 deterministic score → tier → Layer 2 soft criteria → rank within tier → evaluation persisted (criteria, model and prompt versions pinned) → stage transition → CRM notification → shortlist → outreach draft → guards → human approval → send

6.4 Prompt-injection defence

CVs are adversarial input — candidates embed hidden instructions and invisible keyword lists to game screening. Text matching the background colour, text below four points, text outside the page bounds, zero-width and bidirectional-override characters are all detected and stripped before any model call, and recorded on the evaluation. A flagged document goes to human review regardless of score, and the recruiter is shown what was found. Structurally, the scoring model never sees raw document text — only the sanitised, schema-constrained profile.

08

Non-functional requirements

NFR-01

Shared-capacity discipline. The language model host is shared with customer-facing chatbots, one of which is going public at substantial volume. Screening shall run through a globally rate-limited client with a configured concurrency ceiling of four, and batch screening shall default to an overnight window. Both values shall be configuration, not constants in code. Raising the ceiling is not a permitted remedy for insufficient throughput.

MustOwner AI/ML + Infrastructure
NFR-02

No interactive dependency. No production path shall depend on a desktop session, a logged-in browser, or an individual's workstation.

MustOwner AI/ML
NFR-03

Durable, queryable work queue. Screening work shall be recorded durably and be inspectable after the fact. Hiring decisions require a job record that can be audited, not an ephemeral queue.

MustOwner AI/ML
NFR-04

Resumability. A worker failure mid-evaluation shall resume from its last completed step rather than restarting, given the scarcity of the processing window.

MustOwner AI/ML
NFR-05

Observability. Every model call shall be traced with its prompt version, model version and subject record. Logs shall be structured.

MustOwner AI/ML
NFR-06

Horizontal scale where it is needed. Document processing shall scale out independently of model-bound scoring, which stays deliberately constrained.

ShouldOwner AI/ML
NFR-07

Bilingual throughout. Keyword sets, location vocabularies and intent signals shall be maintained in both English and Egyptian Arabic. A change to one language requires the corresponding change to the other.

MustOwner AI/ML
NFR-08

Deterministic reproducibility. Re-running the deterministic layers over an unchanged profile with an unchanged criteria version shall always produce an identical result.

MustOwner AI/ML
NFR-09

Access control. Authentication and role-based authorisation, with candidate visibility scoped by assignment and organisational position, and every administrative action attributed.

Compliance & legal

Timing

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law executive regulations were issued in November 2025 with a one-year grace period. Treat October 2026 as the enforcement date. That is roughly one month after this document's date, and it falls inside the delivery window — compliance requirements are therefore delivery blockers, not follow-up work.

CR-01

Candidate personal data shall remain on infrastructure controlled by the company. No candidate personal data shall be transmitted to any external service, including model APIs, spreadsheet services and third-party application hosts.

MustOwner AI/ML + Legal
CR-02

A lawful basis shall be recorded before any candidate is contacted, and the record shall capture what the candidate agreed to, when, and through which channel.

MustOwner Legal
CR-03

Each candidate shall carry a retention deadline, and expired records shall be purged automatically across every store.

MustOwner AI/ML + Legal
CR-04

A candidate shall be able to obtain the reasons for a decision about them, and the platform shall be able to produce those reasons from stored records without re-processing.

MustOwner AI/ML
CR-05

No automated decision shall have final effect on a candidate without human confirmation.

MustOwner AI/ML + TA
CR-06

The platform shall not acquire candidate data by any means prohibited by a source platform's terms of service, and shall contain no capability whose purpose is to evade detection.

MustOwner AI/ML
CR-07

Criteria shall be reviewed for unlawful or indefensible discrimination before each version is activated, and that review shall be recorded against the version.

MustOwner Legal + Criteria owner
Flagged for legal review

Present criteria include age bands as hard disqualifiers and infer age from graduation year where it is not stated. Age-based screening carries discrimination exposure in many jurisdictions. CR-07 exists to get a documented position on this before the first criteria version is activated — this document does not assume one.

Delivery phases

Phases are sequential and each is independently shippable. The order is chosen so the two highest risks — changing someone's score without noticing, and the unmeasurable funnel — are addressed first.

PhaseDeliversExit criterion
P0Foundation — repository, environments, schemas, configuration, external interfaces with test doubles, health checksAll processes start clean and report healthy
P1Scoring parity. Current scorer ported as a versioned criteria set; full-population replay harnessZero unexplained differences across all 5,140 records. Every difference ruled on by the criteria owner. No later phase begins until this passes
P2Data migration — 47 spreadsheet columns decomposed into structured entities, provenance recorded, unrecorded states made explicit, historic location fix re-scoredCounts reconcile; a 200-record sample passes manual inspection; the re-score difference report is accepted
P3Pipeline and API. Requisitions, applications, enforced stage machine, transition events, assignment, scoping, override with reason, CRM API and event streamA recruiter moves a candidate through the full funnel from the dashboard, and the funnel report is generated from events
P4Intake and extraction — application and prefill API, CV parsing, OCR routing, sanitisation, immutable document storage, archive importA candidate applies from a social link, uploads a CV, and receives an accurate prefilled form
P5Identity — Arabic-aware de-duplication, canonical merge, source history, ambiguity flaggingFalse-merge rate below 0.5% on 500 manually checked pairs
P6Criteria management — rule expressions, gates, versioning, shadow difference reportsGate correctness 100% on the evaluation set; a shadow report is readable by a non-engineer
P7Model layer — soft criteria with evidence, rate-limited client, overnight window, review queue, evaluation harness in continuous integrationAgreement with human labels above the agreed threshold and shared-host latency unchanged during a full batch
P8Retrieval and shortlist — profile summaries, vector index, hybrid retrieval, ranking, employer diversity cap, source reportingA recruiter obtains a ranked shortlist for a live requisition
P9Outreach — consent model, guards, drafting, human-approved sending, contact logging, internal calendar schedulingEvery disallowed case is provably blocked in tests; a full cycle from shortlist to booked interview completes
P10Transition — parallel running, capability-by-capability decommissioning, spreadsheet frozen and archived, criteria performance reportingOne complete hiring cycle runs on the platform alone; the legacy system is retired
Pilot before general release

Before P9, the platform runs in shadow for one brand and one department: it scores, humans decide, and the two are compared. A full hiring cycle completed in shadow — with the comparison reported — is the precondition for enabling outbound contact.

Glossary

TermMeaning in this document
Entry-level trackThe inbound motion targeting recent graduates and junior sales staff. Scored to 100 across location, sales fit, entry level, education and contact. Tiers P1 to P4
Tenured trackThe headhunting motion targeting supervisors, team leaders and sales managers with tenure at competitor firms. Scored to 100 across title fit, employer, tenure, location and move signal. Tiers T1 to T4
Hard gateA deterministic pass-or-fail condition evaluated before any model is called. Failing one ends the evaluation
Criteria versionAn immutable, numbered set of gates, weights and thresholds. Every evaluation records the version that produced it
Shadow reportThe difference produced by re-running a proposed criteria version over already-decided candidates, showing exactly who would change tier
Golden replayRe-running the migrated scoring implementation over every existing record and asserting identical results
ProvenanceThe source, verification state and verification time recorded against an individual candidate field
BorderlineA first-class verdict for candidates near a threshold, surfaced for human review rather than rounded away
Review queueThe human work list holding borderline verdicts, flagged documents, unverified candidates and unresolved ambiguities
Crossover candidateA candidate from a client-facing, sales-trained background outside real estate — premium retail, banking, insurance, telecom — proposed as a scored source. Pending OPN-05
