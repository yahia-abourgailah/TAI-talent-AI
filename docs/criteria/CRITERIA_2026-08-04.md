## Two pipelines, never mixed

TAI runs **two parallel recruiting motions** with different scorecards. Candidates are scored against one or the other — never combined.

| | Track A — Entry Level | Track B — Tenured Movers |
|---|---|---|
| **Source** | Wuzzuf, LinkedIn applicants, LinkedIn personal posts, Facebook hiring groups (TBD) | LinkedIn feed signals, LinkedIn Recruiter targeted, Apollo people search |
| **Target** | Fresh grads + junior sales reps | Supervisors / Team Leaders / Sales Managers at competitors |
| **Age** | 19–35 | 27–40 |
| **Experience** | 0–10 yrs | Tenured at current employer (3+ yrs ★) |
| **Output sheet** | `Entry_Level_Candidates_YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx` | `Tenured_Movers_YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx` |
| **Mode flag** | `score_candidate(c, mode="entry")` | `score_candidate(c, mode="headhunt")` |

### Hard disqualifiers (Track B)
- Title is **too senior**: Director, Head of, VP, Chief, CEO/COO/CFO, President, Owner, Founder, GM, Branch / Area / Regional / Group / Department Manager
- **Not** currently at a real estate / brokerage / property company
- Outside Cairo
- Age **> 40 or < 22** (under-22 routes back to Track A instead)

### Scoring (max 100)
| Component | Max | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Title fit | 25 | See tenured-titles table above |
| Employer | 25 | Confirmed competitor brokerage = **25**. Other RE company = **12**. Non-RE = **DQ** |
| Tenure at current employer | 20 | 4+ yrs = **20 ★**. 3 yrs = **16 ★**. 2 yrs = 10. <2 yrs = 5 + flag *"possible job-hopper"* |
| Location | 15 | Near New Cairo = 15. Cairo / Giza / 6 Oct / 10th Ramadan = 10. Other = 3 |
| Move signal | 15 | `open_to_work` = 15. `engaged_post` (interacted with competitor job posts) = 10. `passive` = 5 |

### Tenured ★ flag
Any candidate with **3+ years at one employer** gets a `★ TENURED` marker in the sheet, regardless of total score. These are prioritized for outreach review.

### Tiers
- **T1** ≥ 80 — Priority Outreach Today  *(corrected 2026-08-04 to match `CLAUDE.md`; was 75)*
- **T2** 60–79 — Warm — Contact This Week  *(corrected 2026-08-04; was 55)*
- **T3** 40–59 — Watchlist  *(corrected 2026-08-04; was 35)*
- **T4** < 40 — Skip

## Sourcing playbook

### Track A — Entry Level (inbound)
1. **Wuzzuf**: Filter for Cairo + sales/customer service + 0–5 yrs. Export to CSV.
   - **Skip "unlocked" candidates** — on Wuzzuf, an unlocked profile means they have been previously contacted. Do not scrape or re-add them to the master sheet.
   - **Quality gate** — only append Wuzzuf candidates if there are genuinely qualified calibers in the batch (P1 or strong P2). Do not pad the sheet with weak matches just to have numbers.
   - **No duplicates** — deduplicate by Wuzzuf ID before appending. Never add the same candidate twice regardless of how many times they appear in different scraping runs.
2. **LinkedIn applicants refilter**: Re-run scorer over existing TAI job applicants.
3. **LinkedIn personal posts**: Look for "looking for opportunity in real estate" posts.
4. **Facebook hiring groups**: HELD pending group list from user.

### Track B — Tenured Movers (headhunt)
1. **LinkedIn feed**: Watch for posts from Sales Managers / Team Leaders at the 22 competitors. Signals: "open to work", anniversary posts, complaints about restructure.
2. **LinkedIn Recruiter**: Boolean search across all 22 competitors for the tenured titles.
3. **Apollo people search**: Pull `current_title IN [tenured titles] AND organization_name IN [competitors] AND location = Cairo, Egypt`.

#### Expanded target industries for Track B headhunting

Beyond the 22 competitor brokerages, the following industries and employers produce candidates who are presentable, client-facing, and sales-trained — a direct match for TAI's profile:

**Retail sales — high-end & luxury (strongest signal)**
Luxury and premium retail brands apply rigorous grooming, presentation, and communication standards in their own hiring — staff are pre-screened for the exact qualities TAI values. Physical interview still required to confirm real estate fit, but the background is a strong positive signal.

Examples: Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Dior, Prada, Burberry, Michael Kors, Coach, Calvin Klein, Tommy Hilfiger, Hugo Boss, Ralph Lauren, Lacoste, Massimo Dutti, Mango, Marks & Spencer, Victoria's Secret, MAC Cosmetics, Mazaya, Charlotte Tilbury, Inglot, Sephora, Faces, Bath & Body Works, The Body Shop, L'Oréal, Estée Lauder, Cartier, Rolex, TAG Heuer, Mont Blanc, Rivoli, Magrabi.

**Retail sales — reputable mainstream chains**
Staff from well-known retail brands are consistently presentable and trained in face-to-face sales. Examples: Zara, H&M, LC Waikiki, Aldo, Pandora, iStyle, Virgin Megastore, automotive showrooms (BMW, Mercedes, Kia, Toyota authorised dealers).

**Banking & wealth management**
Relationship managers, personal bankers, and bancassurance sales at retail banks who deal with high-net-worth clients or property financing (CIB, HSBC Egypt, QNB, Banque Misr, NBE).

**Insurance sales**
Direct sales agents and brokers from Allianz, MetLife, AXA — used to commission-based structures and rejection.

**Telecom sales**
Vodafone, Orange, Etisalat — especially B2B account managers and store sales supervisors.

**Scoring note for Track B expanded industries**
Candidates from the above industries who are not at an RE/brokerage firm should score under the "Employer" dimension as follows:
- Premium retail / tobacco multinationals / tier-1 banks → treat as **"Other RE-adjacent" = 12 pts** (same as Other RE company)
- Standard FMCG / telecom / insurance → **8 pts**
- These candidates are **not disqualified** even though they are not at an RE company — the DQ rule ("Not at an RE company") is relaxed for this expanded set. Flag with `★ CROSSOVER` in the sheet.

### Sourcing tools
- **Claude in Chrome** is the primary tool for LinkedIn Recruiter and feed scanning (user must be logged in).
- **Apollo MCP** (`apollo_mixed_people_api_search`) is the fastest way to populate Track B.
- **No automated scraping** of LinkedIn or Facebook beyond what an authenticated logged-in session can see in real time.

## Recurring task

Schedule both tracks to run **daily** via the `scheduled-tasks` MCP. Each run:
1. Pulls new candidates from each available source.
2. Scores them using the appropriate track.
3. Appends to the day's Excel file in `/Leila AI/TAI/runs/`.
4. Skips duplicates (matched by phone, email, or LinkedIn URL).
5. Posts a summary count to the user.

**Caveat:** Daily runs require LinkedIn / Facebook sessions to remain logged in. Sessions expire every few days; user will need to re-auth.
