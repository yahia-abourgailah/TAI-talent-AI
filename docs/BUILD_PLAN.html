01What we build in 8 weeks

One sentence: a candidate applies on our own page, the system scores them, and a recruiter moves them through real steps that are recorded.

Today this is the problem, from the BRD:

  • Candidate data sits in Google Sheets and on an outside host. The law says this must stop.
  • Candidates are copied from LinkedIn, Facebook and TikTok. Those sites forbid it. The account was flagged once.
  • Only 15 of 5,140 candidates have a hiring step recorded. So no one can measure anything.
  • Everything runs on one laptop. If it is off, work stops.

After 8 weeks, this is the new way a candidate arrives:

We post a job on LinkedIn, TikTok or Facebook.
The post has a link to our own page.
        │
        ▼
Candidate uploads a CV.  ──►  OCR API reads it  ──►  form comes back filled
        │
        ▼
Candidate checks the form and agrees to be contacted.
        │
        ▼
Rules check: Cairo? age? manager? our own staff? ──[no]──► human review
        │                                                   (never rejected by the system)
        ▼
Rules give points out of 100  ──►  tier P1 to P4  ──►  saved forever
        │
        ▼
Recruiter gets the candidate  ──►  moves them step by step  ──►  funnel report

This takes seconds, not hours. The rules are simple maths. No AI is needed to give a tier.

Decide this before we start — where your OCR runs

You have an OCR API to read CVs. We need it in week 5.

If it runs outside the company, every CV leaves the company. That breaks rule CR-01 in the BRD, which is a Must. It is also the exact problem we are trying to fix.

Choose one: (1) run the OCR inside our own network — best, and the plan assumes this. (2) Sign a data agreement with the OCR company and get legal to accept it. (3) Find a different OCR we can host — this costs about one week of Person B.

02Words used in this plan

The BRD uses some heavy words. Here is what each one means, in normal language.

WordWhat it means here
Hard gateA yes/no check before anything else. Example: not in Cairo → stop. No points are counted.
TierThe grade a candidate gets. P1 is the best, P4 is the lowest. T1 to T4 for the senior track.
Criteria versionOne saved copy of all the rules, with a number. Rules are never edited. A change makes version 2.
Golden replayRun the new scorer over all 5,140 old candidates and check every answer is the same as today.
Stage / stage eventThe step a candidate is at (called, interviewed, hired). Each move is saved with who did it and when.
FunnelHow many candidates reach each step. 100 called → 40 replied → 10 interviewed.
ProvenanceWhere a value came from, who checked it, and when. Saved next to the value itself.
BorderlineA candidate close to the line. The system does not guess — a person looks at them.
ConsentThe candidate said yes, we may contact you. Saved with the date and the channel.

What this means on day one of go-live

Because P9 is not built, the platform sends no messages. Recruiters keep calling and writing to candidates the way they do today. Then they record what happened in the system.

This is not a gap in the build. It is the law waiting for an answer. And it still fixes the real problem, because now every call is recorded.

09After week 8

In order of value:

  1. Outreach (P9) — as soon as legal answers OPN-01. Messages, consent guards, a person pressing send, interview booking. This is where the business value of the BRD sits.
  2. AI soft scoring (P7) — ranking inside a tier with evidence, run at night against the shared server.
  3. Shortlists (P8) — ranked lists per job, with a limit per employer.
  4. Readable rule-change reports (P6) — so Karim can review a new version without an engineer.
  5. Rule performance (BR-603) — after about three months of real hiring results exist.

None of these are needed for the platform to be useful. That is the reason to ship the 8 weeks first.
